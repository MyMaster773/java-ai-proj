## Visual language

- Light neutral canvas suited to bright kiosk and back-office environments.
- Navy headings and slate body text; blue is reserved for primary actions and active navigation.
- Panels use thin neutral borders and small radii rather than floating card stacks.
- Data density increases on kitchen and admin screens while touch targets stay at least 44px.
- Status is communicated with text plus color: Placed, Preparing, Ready, and Collected.
